What a Google Business Profile is

This is the card a customer sees before calling you

Someone in your town searches Google for “verbouwing” or “badkamer renovatie”. A map with three companies appears above the results. They click one and see a card like this. That is your Google Business Profile. It is free, but most companies set it up half-heartedly, and it decides whether you make that top three at all.

Questions

Do I need an office or a shop?

No. A Google Business Profile also works without premises, with a hidden address. You need one address you work from, written the same way everywhere.

Do I need a website?

Not for Veni. With Vidi and Vici, pages for your search terms and towns go on your own website or on one built by a partner.

How long does it take?

The profile is ready after 4 weeks, and that is when you see the first movement on the map. A top 3 position usually holds after about 3 months of work.

What do I have to do?

Two things: ask customers for a review and send me photos of your work. I do the rest.

Do you promise first place?

No. With Vidi and Vici I guarantee top 3 for one search term in your home town: in at least 30% of measuring points within 5 km after 90 days. If we don't get there, I keep working for free.
